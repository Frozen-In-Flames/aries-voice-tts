Aries Voice TTS

A custom text-to-speech model trained on the voice of Aries Hilton. The trained model is published in this repository; generated samples from it are below.


Model status: trained and published. A VITS model (Piper architecture) fine-tuned on the artist's own 63 recordings from the Piper en_US-amy-medium base checkpoint, exported to ONNX and published in this repository under model/, and inside the validated voicepack, which now declares status trained. The remaining step is wiring this page's Speak button to run the published model in the browser.

The validated voicepack and the full corpus are public in this repository: aries-hilton.voicepack.
